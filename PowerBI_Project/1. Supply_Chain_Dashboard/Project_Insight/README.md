# 📊 Sales & Supply Chain Analytics Dashboard (Power BI)

## 📌 Overview
This project showcases an interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze sales performance, order trends, shipment efficiency, and regional revenue distribution. The objective is to transform transactional data into actionable insights for improving demand forecasting, logistics performance, and business growth.

---

## 🎯 Objectives
- Analyze order quantity trends over time  
- Evaluate shipment delays and delivery performance  
- Identify top-performing markets by revenue  
- Support data-driven decision making in supply chain and sales  

---

## 🛠 Tools & Technologies
- Power BI (Data Visualization & Dashboard)
- Excel / CSV (Data Source)
- DAX (Data Modeling & Calculations)

---

## 📊 Key Insights

### 1. Order Quantity Trend
- Peak Demand: **8.0K orders (end of 2024)**
- Lowest Range: **4.3K – 4.6K orders**
- Trend: Fluctuating demand with a sharp year-end increase  

👉 The data shows high demand volatility with a strong seasonal spike at the end of the year, indicating potential influence from promotions or peak-season demand.

---

### 2. Shipment Performance
- Delivered Shipments: **2,148K**
- Delayed Shipments: **228K**
- Perfect Shipment Rate: **75%**
- Top Delay Contributors:
  - Maersk (87)
  - DHL (66)
  - DB Schenker (65)

👉 Shipment performance indicates operational inefficiencies, with delays impacting overall service quality and highlighting the need for logistics optimization.

---

### 3. Revenue by Country
- Top Market: **USA (~7bn revenue)**
- Secondary Markets: India, Germany, South Korea  
- Total Revenue: **Rp16bn**

👉 The business is highly dependent on the USA market, creating potential risk exposure and highlighting the need for market diversification.

---

## 💡 Business Value
- Improves demand forecasting and inventory planning  
- Identifies logistics inefficiencies and delay risks  
- Supports strategic expansion in underperforming markets  
- Enables data-driven decisions in supply chain and sales operations
