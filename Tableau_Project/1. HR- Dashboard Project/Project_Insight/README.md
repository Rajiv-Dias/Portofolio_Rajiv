# 📊 Workforce Analytics Dashboard

## 📌 Overview
This project presents a comprehensive Workforce Analytics Dashboard designed to analyze employee distribution, retention, demographics, and compensation patterns. The goal is to transform raw HR data into actionable insights that support strategic workforce planning and decision-making.

---

## 🎯 Objectives
- Analyze workforce composition and turnover trends  
- Identify department-level employee concentration  
- Understand workforce demographics and age distribution  
- Evaluate salary patterns across roles and seniority levels  
- Assess workforce distribution across locations  

---

## 🛠 Tools & Technologies
- Tableau (Data Visualization)
- SQL (Data Analysis)
- Excel / CSV (Data Source)

---

## 📊 Key Insights

### 1. Workforce Composition & Retention

![imagealt](https://github.com/Rajiv-Dias/Portofolio_Rajiv/blob/bcf80493d8b1f029105df68e625ab51ebe3d774a/Tableau_Project/1.%20HR-%20Dashboard%20Project/Project_Insight/Project_Insight/HR_Analysis-1.png)

- Total Employees: **8,950**
- Active Employees: **7,984**
- Turnover Rate: **10.79%**

👉 The organization maintains a relatively stable workforce, with approximately 1 in 10 employees leaving within the observed period. Hiring trends remain higher than terminations, indicating ongoing workforce expansion or replacement.

---

### 2. Workforce Concentration by Department
- Operations: **2,429 employees**
- Sales: **1,634 employees**
- Customer Service: **1,489 employees**
- IT: **1,243 employees**

👉 The workforce is highly concentrated in operations and customer-facing roles, indicating an operationally intensive business model with strong service orientation.

---

### 3. Workforce Demographics
- Largest Age Group: **35–44 years old**
- Average Age: **42.43 years**

👉 The organization is dominated by mid-career professionals, which indicates strong experience but also potential future retirement risks and the need for younger talent pipelines.

---

### 4. Salary vs Seniority
- Finance Manager: ~**$120K**
- IT Manager: ~**$110K**
- Sales Manager: ~**$105K**
- Entry-level roles: ~**$58K–$60K**

👉 There is a clear positive correlation between experience, role level, and salary, reflecting a structured compensation hierarchy.

---

### 5. Workforce Distribution by Location
- Headquarters: **6,270 employees**
- Branches: **2,680 employees**

👉 The company operates with a highly centralized structure, with most employees located at headquarters, indicating centralized decision-making and operations.

---

## 💡 Business Value
- Supports HR in monitoring turnover and retention risks  
- Helps identify high-demand departments for hiring strategies  
- Provides insights for workforce planning and succession management  
- Enables data-driven compensation and organizational decisions
